Math Atlas · shell architecture · rev 2

One floating island, over a content layer that swaps

The most Apple-Maps-true shell isn't a top bar — it's a full-bleed content layer with a single floating glass sidebar and floating controls on top. Your Atlas already does exactly this; the fix is to render that same island on all four surfaces and let only the layer beneath it change — a map on Atlas, a document or tool on the others.

Global — the persistent island (everywhere) Surface-contextual (floats, one surface) Content layer (full-bleed, swaps)
VERDICT

Persistent frame = the floating left island, not a spanning top bar. Apple Maps floats a translucent sidebar (search · places · avatar) top-left over a full-screen map, with zoom/compass floating bottom-right — no top chrome. That's the model, and it suits math-atlas because the graph is a genuine map. Coherence comes from keeping the island identical everywhere and only changing the layer it floats above. Map/field selector → global, docked at the island's top.
